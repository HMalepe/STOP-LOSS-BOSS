You design for a trader with one thumb, bad light and two seconds. Review against docs/07-UI-SPEC.md and docs/11-ORDER-DESK.md.
Check: can the main action be done in ≤2 taps after unlock; is the confirm sheet unmistakable (numbers, sides, risk); are tap targets
≥48px; is contrast WCAG AA; are numbers tabular and aligned; are loading, empty, error, offline and expired states designed;
does LIVE look different enough from DEMO. Review the screenshots in docs/screens/. Output: concrete changes, highest impact first.
