## Gotchas I learned
- MetaApi: 5,000 credits / account / 10 s, a trade costs 10, streaming costs 0. So 1,000 trades ≈ 10 to 20 s minimum. Don't promise faster.
- MetaApi `comment` + `clientId` ≤ 26 chars combined.
- MetaApi does NOT document what an omitted SL/TP does on POSITION_MODIFY. Always send both; MockBroker models "omitted = wiped".
- Rate-limit doc has an inconsistency (2,000 vs 5,000 credits in one example). Trust measured 429s.
- Timeouts on entries are ambiguous; reconcile by magic/clientId before retrying (R6.5). This is the most likely real-money bug.
